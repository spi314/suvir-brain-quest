How to play: read each problem twice, find the hidden multiplication first, then finish the second step. Try it yourself before tapping Show hint. Draw a bar model on paper if it helps. Type your answer and tap Check.

1Seven bags hold 8 marbles each. Suvir gives away 9 marbles. How many marbles does he have left?
Show hint

Multiply first: 7 groups of 8. Then take away 9.

2The library has 6 shelves with 12 books on each shelf. 15 books are checked out. How many books are still on the shelves?
Show hint

6 groups of 12, then subtract the checked-out books.

3Suvir scores 9 rounds of 6 points in a game, then loses 8 points. What is his final score?
Show hint

9 groups of 6, then subtract 8.

4Suvir goes to 5 soccer practices a week for 4 weeks, plus 3 extra Saturday practices. How many practices is that in all?
Show hint

Multiply 5 by 4, then add the 3 extra.

5Movie tickets cost $7 each. Suvir buys 8 tickets and pays with $60. How much change does he get?
Show hint

8 groups of 7, then subtract from 60.

6A bus has 11 rows with 5 seats in each row, but 4 seats are broken. How many seats can be used?
Show hint

11 groups of 5, then subtract the 4 broken seats.

7Three boxes hold 9 apples each, and 6 more apples sit in a bowl. How many apples are there in all?
Show hint

3 groups of 9, then add 6.

8Suvir reads 9 pages every day for 7 days. His goal is 80 pages. How many pages short of his goal is he?

13Suvir saves $9 every week for 6 weeks, then spends $25 on a book. How much money does he have left?
Show hint

6 groups of 9, then subtract 25.

14A sticker album holds 8 rows of 7 stickers. Suvir has placed 14 stickers. How many spots are still empty?

8 groups of 7, then subtract 14.

15Five packs hold 11 trading cards each, and Suvir gets 9 more cards for his birthday. How many cards does he have now?

5 groups of 11, then add 9.

16A 96-page book is split into 8 equal chapters. How many pages are in each chapter?
Show hint

Think: 8 times what number makes 96?

17Seven cartons hold 12 eggs each. A baker uses 30 eggs. How many eggs are left?

7 groups of 12, then subtract 30.

18A theater has 9 rows of 9 seats. 65 people are seated. How many seats are still empty?
Show hint

9 groups of 9, then subtract 65.
