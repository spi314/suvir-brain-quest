Noetic Targeted Skills Practice - Set 1  •  Name: ______________________  •  Date: ______________  •  Score: ______ / 10
10 problems • take your time • show your work in the space under each problem.
1Mr. Patel cuts one long ribbon into 6 pieces for art projects. How many cuts did he make?
2The science fair is open every day from March 4 through March 11, including both of those days. How many days is it open?
34 friends start a chess club. Each of them teaches 3 new kids to play chess. Nobody leaves the club. How many kids are in the chess club now?
4Mia has 3 juice flavors: apple, grape, and orange. She picks one flavor for Monday and a different flavor for Tuesday. How many different ways can she pick?
5There are 20 kids in the gym. 9 kids are wearing hats (4 of them are also wearing gloves). 5 other kids are wearing only gloves and no hat. How many kids are wearing neither a hat nor gloves?
6Red team players wear the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4. Blue team players wear 1, 2, 3, 4. A red player and a blue player shake hands when their two numbers add to an even number. How many handshakes happen?
7Nina folds a paper strip in half, then cuts straight across the middle of the folded strip. When she opens it up, how many pieces of paper does she have?
8Suvir's reading streak is 9 days long, including the last day. The last day is Friday, May 15. On what date did his streak start?
9A magic bean plant doubles its height every day. On Day 6 it reaches the top of the fence. On which day was it half as tall as the top of the fence?
1015 kids voted on snacks. 8 kids like pizza, 9 kids like tacos, and 4 kids like both. How many kids like neither pizza nor tacos?
Noetic Targeted Skills Practice - Set 2  •  Name: ______________________  •  Date: ______________  •  Score: ______ / 10
10 problems • take your time • show your work in the space under each problem.
1A wooden log is sawed into 5 pieces. Each saw cut takes 3 minutes. How many minutes of sawing is that in all?
2The library reading challenge starts June 20 and lasts 14 days in a row, including June 20. What is the date of the last day? (June has 30 days.)
3On Monday, 5 kids know a song. On Tuesday, each of them teaches the song to 2 new kids. How many kids know the song after Tuesday?
4Ana, Bo, and Cy line up for a photo. How many different line-ups can they make?
5A baker decorates 25 cupcakes. 12 cupcakes get sprinkles (5 of them also get cherries). 6 other cupcakes get only cherries and no sprinkles. How many cupcakes get neither sprinkles nor cherries?
6Two piles of number cards each have cards numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. You pick one card from each pile. You win a point if the two numbers add to an odd number. How many winning pairs of cards are there?
7Leo has 8 strips of paper. He cuts some of the strips in half. Now he has 13 strips of paper. How many strips did he cut?
8Summer camp starts on Wednesday, August 5 and runs 7 days in a row, including August 5. What is the date of the last day of camp?
9A secret code is one letter (A, B, or C) followed by one digit (1, 2, 3, or 4). How many different codes are there?
1022 students joined after-school clubs. 11 joined the art club, 9 joined the chess club, and 4 joined both clubs. How many students joined neither club?
Noetic Targeted Skills Practice - Set 3  •  Name: ______________________  •  Date: ______________  •  Score: ______ / 10
10 problems • take your time • show your work in the space under each problem.
1A chef makes 4 straight cuts all the way across a round pizza, and every cut passes through the center. How many slices does the pizza have?
2A book sale opens Monday, November 9 and closes Saturday, November 14. It is open every day, including the first and the last day. How many days is the book sale open?
3Farmer Jo has 6 hens. Each hen hatches 5 chicks. How many birds does Farmer Jo have on the farm now?
4There are 5 different picture books on the shelf. Suvir picks one book to read on Saturday and a different book on Sunday. How many different ways can he pick?
5There are 30 books on a library cart. 14 are comic books (6 of the comics are in Spanish). 7 other books are in Spanish but are not comics. How many books are neither comics nor in Spanish?
6Team A players wear the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. Team B players wear 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. A Team A player and a Team B player high-five when their numbers add to an even number. How many high-fives happen?
7Sam folds a paper strip in half, then folds it in half again the same way. He makes one straight cut through all the layers at the middle of the folded strip. When he opens it up, how many pieces does he have?
8The last day of a 12-day swim class was July 18. The class met every day, including the last day. On what date was the first day of the class?
9A colony of ants doubles in number every day. On Day 7 there are 640 ants. On which day were there 160 ants?
10Spinner X and Spinner Y each have the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. A turn scores a point when the two spun numbers add to an odd number. How many scoring pairs are there?
